WORDLE

How to play

Guess the word in six tries.

Each guess must be a valid five-letter word. The color of the tiles will change to show how close your guess was.

Examples

WEARY

W is in the word and in the correct spot.

PILLS

I is in the word but in the wrong spot.

VAGUE

U is not in the word.
